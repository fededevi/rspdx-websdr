Strip under the spectrum (SSTV search, 2.5 kHz tiles): grey = varying signal, orange = steady but not SSTV, purple = SSTV candidate, teal = FT8, blue = FT4 (found from the slot timing). Purple bands on the waterfall = SSTV decoders in memory. Click the waterfall to tune. In SSB, click the middle of the signal: the receiver then finds the carrier in about 5 s. Use the mouse wheel or the ±10 buttons to correct the pitch by ear. The 200 kHz grid of strong lines is local interference.
